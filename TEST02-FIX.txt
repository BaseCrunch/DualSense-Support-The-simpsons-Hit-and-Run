TEST 02 FIX
===========
Observed Lucas 1.27.1 error:
  Could not load the mod 'Nexus DualSense Prompts'. The required hack
  'Nexus DualSense' does not support being required by a mod.

Cause:
  Test 01 prompt Meta.ini contained RequiredHack=NexusDualSense.

Fix:
  Removed RequiredHack=NexusDualSense.
  Kept RequiredHack=CustomFiles.
  Nexus DualSense and Nexus DualSense Prompts are enabled as two separate
  Lucas checkboxes.

No native controller/haptics code was changed in this patch.
